## 2. The four steps

**Label** — The Process
**H2** — Four Steps, and We Do *Most* of Them

### 01 · Choose a design
**Body** — Browse the forty designs and pick the one that feels right — a heritage flag, a football
crest, a favourite colour, or something quiet and classic. If nothing fits, tell us what you have in
mind and we will draw something for you. If you are not sure, call us and we will talk it through.

### 02 · Send us the details
**Body** — Names, dates, the order of service, hymns, tributes, and the photographs you would like
included. Use the form, or simply WhatsApp everything to us — most families do. You do not need it
all at once, and you do not need to have decided everything.

### 03 · Approve your proof
**Body** — We design the booklet and send your proof back in 24–48 hours, by email or WhatsApp,
whichever suits you. Read it carefully, check every name and date, and tell us what to change. We
will keep revising until it is right. **Nothing is printed until you say yes.**

### 04 · Printed and delivered
**Body** — Once you approve, we print. Booklets go out for next-day UK delivery, or same-day if you
are local — to your home, your church, or straight to your funeral director.

## 4. What we need from you

**Label** — What We Need
**H2** — What to Send, and What Can *Wait*

**Body** — You can start with very little. This is what we eventually need, roughly in order of how
much it holds things up.

| We need | When | If you do not have it |
|---|---|---|
| Their full name, and the date of the service | To begin | Call us anyway — we can hold a slot |
| A photograph for the front cover | Early | Send the best you have; we can retouch and crop |
| The order of service | Before the proof | We can send you a template to fill in |
| Hymns, readings, poems | Before the proof | Choose from our library, or we will suggest some |
| Tributes, the eulogy, acknowledgements | Before printing | These often arrive last. That is normal |
| Gallery photographs | Before printing | Family can WhatsApp them to us directly |

**Body** — Photographs are the thing that most often delays an order, because they have to be
gathered from relatives. Start asking early, and send them to us as they arrive rather than waiting
to have them all.

## 5. Your proof

**Label** — The Proof
**H2** — Nothing Is Printed Until You Say *Yes*

**Body** — Your proof is a full preview of the finished booklet, sent as a PDF by email or WhatsApp.
It is free, and there is no limit on how many changes you ask for.

**Body** — Please check every name, every date and every spelling — including middle names and
nicknames. Once printed, a booklet cannot be corrected, and these are kept for years. If you can,
have a second person in the family read it too. It is much easier to spot a wrong date when you have
not been staring at it.

## 6. If the funeral is very soon

**Label** — Express Orders
**H2** — If You Only Have a Few *Days*

**Body** — Tell us the date of the service in your very first message. We will tell you honestly
whether we can do it, and we will not take an order we cannot deliver in time.

**Body** — Where the service falls inside our standard turnaround, we treat the order as express and
move it to the front of the queue. Express orders carry a **50% surcharge** on the total. We will
always tell you before anything is agreed, and the total will be confirmed in writing.

**Body** — If we genuinely cannot make your date, we will say so and suggest what else might work —
fewer pages, a shorter run, or a digital obituary to share while the booklets follow.

## 7. FAQ block

**H2** — Questions Families Usually *Ask*

Rescued from the nine topics currently rendering as broken markup on `/faqs/`. Answers are written
in the 40–60 word answer-first shape from `../_strategy/09-AEO-GEO.md` so they can win answer boxes.

**How many pages should an order of service be?**
Most funeral order of service booklets are 4 or 8 pages. A 4-page booklet holds the order of service
and one or two photographs. An 8-page booklet adds hymns, a tribute and a small photo gallery. Larger
services often use 12 to 20 pages. We print 4, 8, 12, 16 and 20-page booklets from £122 for 50.

**What size is a funeral order of service?**
Ours are A5 — 148 × 210mm — which is the standard size for UK funeral booklets. It sits comfortably
in one hand, fits a coat pocket to take home, and is large enough for photographs to be seen properly
from a pew.

**How many booklets should we order?**
Order slightly more than you expect to need. Families almost always underestimate, and a reprint is
slower and costs more than the extra copies would have. If you expect 80 people, order 100. Extra
copies are £5.50 each.

**Can you use a photograph from a phone?**
Usually, yes. A photograph taken on any recent phone is normally good enough for print. Older
scanned or printed photographs can also be used, and we can retouch, repair and remove backgrounds.
Send us what you have and we will tell you honestly if it will not work.

**What if we do not know the order of service yet?**
That is very common, and it does not stop us starting. Send us the name, the date and a cover
photograph and we will begin the design. We can send you a template for the running order to fill in,
and the minister or funeral director will usually help you complete it.

**Can we include hymns if we do not know which ones?**
Yes. We have a library of hymns, poems, prayers and readings you can choose from, and we can set the
full words into the booklet so guests can sing along. If you would rather, tell us something about
your loved one and we will suggest a few.

**Do you deliver to the church or the funeral director?**
Yes — wherever is most useful. We deliver to homes, churches, crematoria and funeral directors
across the UK, with same-day delivery available locally. Tell us where and when, and we will arrange
it.

**Do you offer trade prices for funeral directors?**
Yes. We work with funeral directors and churches on trade pricing, with white-label proofs and a
dedicated phone line. See our Partners page or call us to discuss terms.

**What is a Motion Obituary?**
A Motion Obituary is a short animated announcement of the service, made to be shared on WhatsApp and
social media. It carries their photograph, the date, time and place, and it saves ringing round
everybody individually. Ours start at £50 and are produced within 24 hours.
