## 10. FAQ block

**Can we order just one thing?**
Yes. Every product here can be ordered on its own — many families start with the obituary
announcement while they are still deciding on the booklet.

**Will everything match?**
Yes, if you order it from us. The banner, bookmarks, announcements and booklet all use the same design
and colours, so they read as one set.

**Can you match a design we have already had printed elsewhere?**
Usually. Send us a photograph of it and we will tell you honestly whether we can match it.

**How many booklets should we order?**
Order more than you expect to need — families almost always underestimate, and a reprint costs more
and takes longer than the extra copies would have. Extra copies are £5.50 each.
