## 3. How it works for you

**H2** — How It Works for *You*

1. **You send us the details.** Email, WhatsApp, or a phone call. Whatever the family has given you —
   we are used to incomplete information.
2. **We design it and send you a proof in 24–48 hours.** Unbranded if you want it that way.
3. **You show the family.** Changes come back through you, or straight from them if that is easier —
   whichever you prefer.
4. **We print and deliver.** To you, to the family, to the church, or to the crematorium.

**Body** — If a service has been brought forward and you need something the same week, say so when you
call. We will tell you honestly whether we can do it.

## 6. Trade enquiry

**H2** — Let's Talk *Terms*

**Body** — Tell us roughly how many services you arrange a month and what you currently pay, and we
will come back with terms. No obligation, and we will tell you if we are not the cheapest — sometimes
we are not.

**Form fields** — Your name · Company · Role · Phone · Email · Roughly how many services a month ·
What you print today

**CTA** — `Send enquiry`

> A **separate route from the family order form.** A funeral director should never be dropped into a
> 20-screen Typeform written for the bereaved. Different form, different tone, different data.
>
> ⚠️ *"we will tell you if we are not the cheapest"* is a real differentiator and a real commitment.
> Confirm Ashley is happy to say it — it works precisely because most suppliers would not.
