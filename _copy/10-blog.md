### Article 1 — What Is a Nine Night?
**Slug:** `/blog/what-is-a-nine-night/` · **~1,400 words** · **Write this first**

**Answer-first opening**
> A Nine Night is a Caribbean wake held on the ninth night after a death, most closely associated
> with Jamaica. Family and friends gather at the family home to eat, drink, sing, share stories and
> see the person off. It is traditionally the night the spirit departs, and it is a celebration as
> much as a mourning.

**Sections** — Where the tradition comes from · What happens on the night · What is usually served ·
The music · What to bring, and what to wear · How it sits alongside the funeral service · Nine Night
in the UK today · Printed announcements for a Nine Night

**Closing CTA** — *We design Nine Night announcements to match the order of service booklet, so
everything looks like it belongs together.* → `/our-products/`

> **Why first:** no UK funeral printer owns this. It has real search demand from Caribbean families,
> it is the clearest possible proof that Memories understands the send-off rather than just the
> printing, and the brief confirms ninth night announcements are a product line. It is the best AEO
> and GEO opportunity on the whole site — see `../_strategy/09-AEO-GEO.md`.
>
> ## ⛔ Do not publish this without Ashley reading it
>
> This is somebody's culture, not a content-marketing topic. Written well it earns real trust;
> written by someone guessing, it is worse than saying nothing. **Ashley — or a family member —
> must review every claim.** I can draft it; I cannot be the authority on it.

### Article 2 — What to Write in a Funeral Order of Service
**Slug:** `/blog/what-to-write-in-a-funeral-order-of-service/` · **~1,600 words**

**Answer-first opening**
> A funeral order of service usually contains the front cover with their name and dates, the running
> order of the service, the words to the hymns, the readings, a short life story and
> acknowledgements. Most are four or eight pages. Below is the usual shape, with wording you can copy.

**Sections** — The front cover · The running order line by line · Writing a life story (with two
worked examples, short and long) · Writing a tribute · Acknowledgements · The back cover · Common
mistakes — wrong dates, missing middle names, forgetting to thank the hospital

> **Content already exists.** The nine FAQ topics on `/faqs/` are ready-written H2s covering exactly
> this — they are currently rendering as **literal broken markup** because someone pasted HTML with
> smart quotes into the WordPress editor. This is the cheapest content win on the site: rescue, edit,
> publish. Cross-link with the Hymns & Resources guide rather than duplicating it.

### Article 3 — How to Choose Hymns for a Funeral
**Slug:** `/blog/choosing-hymns-for-a-funeral/` · **~1,200 words**

**Answer-first opening**
> Most funerals include two or three hymns — one near the beginning, one after the tribute, and one at
> the end. The most requested in the UK are *How Great Thou Art*, *Amazing Grace* and *The Old Rugged
> Cross*. Choose ones the congregation will know well enough to sing.

**Sections** — How many, and where they sit · Hymns people can actually sing · Traditional versus
gospel · Choosing for someone who was not religious · Printing the words so guests can join in ·
Twenty hymns families ask for most

**CTA** — → `/hymns-and-resources/`

### Article 4 — Bereavement Benefits: What You Can Claim
**Slug:** `/blog/uk-bereavement-benefits/` · **~1,500 words**

**Answer-first opening**
> After a death in the UK you may be able to claim Bereavement Support Payment, and a Funeral
> Expenses Payment if you are on certain benefits. Both are claimed from the DWP. Bereavement Support
> Payment must usually be claimed within 21 months. Check current rates on GOV.UK.

**Sections** — Bereavement Support Payment · Funeral Expenses Payment · Who can claim · The BB1 form
· Deadlines · What to do first · Where to get free help

> ⚠️ Overlaps the Hymns & Resources guidance page. **Decide one canonical home** — recommend the
> resource page is canonical and this article is a shorter, newsier summary linking to it. Two pages
> targeting "bereavement benefits UK" would cannibalise each other.
>
> ## 🔴 Same accuracy warning as the resource page
>
> Real money, real deadlines. Every figure needs a GOV.UK citation and a visible "last checked" date.
> The existing 3,853 words date from 2020 and are almost certainly out of date. Do not carry them
> across unchecked.

## 3. Article template

| Slot | Content |
|---|---|
| H1 | The question, phrased as a person would type it |
| Standfirst | One sentence on what the article answers |
| Byline | Author name · published date · **last updated date** |
| Answer block | 40–60 words, complete on its own, before any elaboration |
| Body | `h2`s phrased as questions |
| Soft CTA | One only, contextual, never a hard sell |
| Related | 2–3 articles + 1 relevant design or product |

**Standing CTA wording** — *We design order of service booklets for families across the UK, by hand,
with your proof in 24–48 hours.* → `/order/`
