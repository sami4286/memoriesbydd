## 2. Ashley's story

**H2** — A Tribute Worthy of *Them*

**Body** — Ashley Whittick lost his mother when he was seven months old. He grew up with her
photograph rather than her, and with the particular kind of absence that never quite becomes normal.

**Body** — Years later, working around funerals, something began to bother him. Families would pull
out all the bells and whistles for a wedding — the stationery, the flowers, months of planning — and
then send someone they had loved for fifty years off with cheap black and white photocopied paper,
folded in half.

**Body** — It was not about money. Most of the families he met would have paid for something better
if anyone had offered it, or shown them what was possible, or simply had the time to sit with them and
ask what their mother had actually been like.

**Body** — So that is what Memories does. Every booklet is designed by hand by someone who has read
what you sent and looked at the photographs. There is a person on the end of the phone, from the first
call to the last delivery. And nothing is printed until you have seen it and said yes.

**Pull quote** — *"I lost my mother at seven months old. What frustrated me was watching families pull
out all the bells and whistles for a wedding, then send their loved ones off with cheap black and
white photocopied paper."*
— **Ashley Whittick**, founder

## 3. What we believe

**H2** — What We *Believe*

| H3 | Body |
|---|---|
| **A booklet is kept** | Nobody throws away an order of service. It goes in a drawer, a Bible, a box of photographs, and it comes out again at anniversaries. It should be made like something that will be looked at in twenty years, because it will be. |

>
> The three testimonials currently on the approved homepage are **invented** — quotes, locations and
> star ratings alike. They must not be reused here or anywhere.

> Under the **Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024** publishing consumer reviews that

>
> **What is needed:** two or three real quotes with written permission, ideally a first name and a
