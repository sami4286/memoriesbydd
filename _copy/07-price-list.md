## 4. How many pages will we need?

**H2** — How Many Pages Will We *Need*?

**Body** — You do not have to work this out yourself — we will tell you when we see what you are
including. But if you want to estimate, pages are counted like this.

| What goes in | Pages |
|---|---|
| Front cover | 1, always included |
| The running order | 1, always included |
| Each hymn | 1 |
| Each tribute | 1 |
| Each reading or poem | 1 |
| Photo gallery, up to 20 photographs | 1, included |
| Every further 20 photographs | 1 more, **£35** |

**Worked example** — A cover, a running order, three hymns, one tribute, one reading and twenty
photographs comes to eight pages. Fifty copies of an eight-page booklet is £267.

**Body** — Booklets are printed in fours, so a nine-page booklet is priced as twelve. We will always
round in your favour where we can.

> ⛔ **All of this is from the brief's automation logic, not from anything published today.** The
> current price list explains none of it, and the £35 extra-gallery charge appears nowhere on the
> live site. Needs confirming before publishing — but publishing *something* here is important,
> because "how is this priced" is the question the current page fails to answer.
>
> ⚠️ "printed in fours" and "round in your favour" are both inferences. Confirm or cut.

**H2** — If the Funeral Is *Soon*

**Body** — Tell us the date of the service in your first message. We will be honest about whether we
can make it, and we will not take an order we cannot deliver in time.

**Body** — Where the service falls inside our normal turnaround, the order is treated as express and

anything is agreed, and confirm the total in writing.

**Body** — Your proof still comes back in 24–48 hours either way. Express affects printing and
