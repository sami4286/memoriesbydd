Funeral booklet FAQs

Clear answers, before you need to ask.

Grief leaves little room for uncertainty. These are the practical answers families most often need before design, proof and print.

What to expect

A calmer path from photographs to the service.

If the funeral is very soon, call us first. We will tell you honestly what can be produced and delivered in time.

What do I need before I begin?

Only the person’s name and a way for us to contact you. The date, design, photographs, hymns and service details can be added as they become available.

How many photographs can I include?

The right number depends on the page count and design. A shorter booklet keeps the gallery concise; a longer booklet can hold a fuller life story and more photographs.

What image quality is best for print?

Original JPEG or PNG files are best. Send the highest-quality versions available rather than screenshots. If an older photograph is all you have, send it—we will explain what can be improved.

How quickly will I receive a proof?

The first digital proof normally takes 24–48 hours after we receive the essential content. Printing and delivery timing is confirmed around the service date.

Can I request changes before printing?

Yes. The proof stage exists so names, dates, wording, photographs and page order can be checked before anything is printed.

Can several relatives send material?

Yes. Material can come from more than one family member. We will help organise duplicates, identify missing details and keep one clear proofing conversation.

Do you deliver throughout the UK?

Yes. We provide nationwide UK delivery. The delivery method and deadline are agreed before print approval so the family knows exactly what to expect.

Can a funeral director order for a family?

Yes. Funeral directors, churches and celebrants can submit the material, receive the proof, or invite the family into the approval process.
