For Funeral Directors

Common Questions

Can I send photographs on WhatsApp?

Yes, and most families do. Send them to 07552 91 6060 with the name of your loved one and we will match them to your order.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Call or message any time and we will take it from there. If you would like to visit, please ring first.

How quickly will you reply?

As quickly as we can. If there is a date for the service, say so when you call or message and we will put you first.

Do you print outside London?

Yes. We deliver across the UK, next-day as standard, and same-day if you are near Stanmore.
