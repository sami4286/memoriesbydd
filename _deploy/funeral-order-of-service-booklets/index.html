Built for the service. Kept for much longer.

An order of service booklet helps guests follow the day, but its lasting value is emotional. It becomes a record of the photographs, words, songs and small details that a family chose together.

Formats and finish

A5 is the most popular finished size. Choose a simple 4-page folded booklet or a stapled multi-page booklet with space for a biography, tributes and larger photo galleries. The right format depends on the service and how much of their story you want to include.

Proof before print

Your first digital proof is normally prepared in 24–48 hours once the essential content has been received. You can request changes before approval. Printing begins only after you have seen the complete design and said yes.
