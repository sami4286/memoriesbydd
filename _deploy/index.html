02 / The memory behind Memories

It began with one photograph—and all the stories it could not tell.

Our founder lost his mother when he was only seven months old. Growing up with questions about the woman she had been made him notice how easily a whole life could be reduced to a few rushed pages.

So every family receives what he wished he had: time, attention, and something beautiful to keep.
