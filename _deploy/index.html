Our Story

A Tribute Worthy of Them

Ashley lost his mother at seven months old. What stayed with him was watching families pull out all the bells and whistles for a wedding, then send their loved ones off with cheap black and white photocopied paper.

Memories exists because of her — so that every family can give someone a send-off with a little class, without it costing the earth.
