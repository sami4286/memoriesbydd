Questions families ask

Answers before you need to ask.

If the funeral is very soon, call first. We will tell you honestly what can be delivered in time.

What do I need before I begin?

Only the person's name and a way for us to contact you. The date, design, photos and service details can be added as they become available.

How many photographs can I include?

It depends on the design and page count. A four-page booklet keeps the gallery concise; longer booklets can hold a fuller life story and larger photo collection.

Can different relatives send photographs?

Yes. We can receive items from more than one person and help identify duplicates or missing details.

What if I notice a mistake in the proof?

Tell us. The proof stage exists to catch wording, dates, names and image choices before print.

How quickly can you deliver?

The first design proof normally takes 24–48 hours after the essential content is received. Printing and delivery time depends on the approved format, quantity, location and deadline. Where we accept an express turnaround, a 50% surcharge is confirmed before design begins.
