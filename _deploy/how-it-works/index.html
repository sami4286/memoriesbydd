How It Works

From First Call to Delivered

Most families come to us having never arranged a funeral before, often with only a few days to do it. This page explains exactly what happens, in what order, and what we need from you — so there are no surprises at a time when you have had enough of those.

The Process

Four Steps, and We Do Most of Them

01

Choose a design

Browse the forty designs and pick the one that feels right — a heritage flag, a football crest, a favourite colour, or something quiet and classic.

If nothing fits, tell us what you have in mind and we will draw something for you. That costs no more. If you are not sure, call and we will talk it through — we can usually narrow forty down to three in about five minutes.

Browse the designs
02

Send us the details

Names, dates, the order of service, hymns, tributes, and the photographs you would like included. Use the form, or simply WhatsApp everything to us — most families do.

You do not need it all at once, and you do not need to have decided everything. Start with a name and the date of the service and we can begin.

WhatsApp your photos
03

Approve your proof

We design the booklet and send your proof back in 24–48 hours, by email or WhatsApp, whichever suits you.

Read it carefully, check every name and date, and tell us what to change. We will keep revising until it is right. Nothing is printed until you say yes.

04

Printed and delivered

Once you approve, we print. Booklets go out for next-day UK delivery, or same-day if you are local — to your home, your church, or straight to your funeral director.

The Proof

Nothing Is Printed Until You Say Yes

Your proof is a full preview of the finished booklet, sent as a PDF by email or WhatsApp. It is free, and there is no limit on how many changes you ask for.

Please check every name, every date and every spelling — including middle names and nicknames.

Express Orders

If You Only Have a Few Days

Tell us the date of the service in your very first message. We will tell you honestly whether we can do it, and we will not take an order we cannot deliver in time.

Where the service falls inside our standard turnaround, we treat the order as express and move it to the front of the queue. Express orders carry a 50% surcharge on the total. We will always tell you before anything is agreed, and the total will be confirmed in writing.

If we genuinely cannot make your date, we will say so and suggest what else might work — fewer pages, a shorter run, or an obituary announcement to share while the booklets follow.

Questions

Questions Families Usually Ask

How many pages should an order of service be?

Most funeral order of service booklets are 4 or 8 pages. A 4-page booklet holds the order of service and one or two photographs. An 8-page booklet adds hymns, a tribute and a small photo gallery. Larger services often use 12 to 20 pages. We print 4, 8, 12, 16 and 20-page booklets from £122 for 50.

What size is a funeral order of service?

Ours are A5 — 148 × 210mm — which is the standard size for UK funeral booklets. It sits comfortably in one hand, fits a coat pocket to take home, and is large enough for photographs to be seen properly from a pew.

How many booklets should we order?

Order slightly more than you expect to need. Families almost always underestimate, and a reprint is slower and costs more than the extra copies would have. If you expect 80 people, order 100. Extra copies are £5.50 each.

Can you use a photograph from a phone?

Usually, yes. A photograph taken on any recent phone is normally good enough for print. Older scanned or printed photographs can also be used, and we can retouch, repair and remove backgrounds. Send us what you have and we will tell you honestly if it will not work.

What if we do not know the order of service yet?

That is very common, and it does not stop us starting. Send us the name, the date and a cover photograph and we will begin the design. We can send you a template for the running order to fill in, and the minister or funeral director will usually help you complete it.

Can we include hymns if we do not know which ones?

Yes. We have a library of hymns, poems, prayers and readings you can choose from, and we can set the full words into the booklet so guests can sing along. If you would rather, tell us something about your loved one and we will suggest a few.

Do you deliver to the church or the funeral director?

Yes — wherever is most useful. We deliver to homes, churches, crematoria and funeral directors across the UK, with same-day delivery available locally. Tell us where and when, and we will arrange it.

Do you offer trade prices for funeral directors?

Yes. We work with funeral directors and churches on trade pricing, with white-label proofs and a dedicated phone line. See our terms for funeral directors or call us to discuss.

What is a Motion Obituary?

A short animated announcement of the service, made to be shared on WhatsApp and social media. It carries their photograph, the date, time and place, and it saves ringing round everybody individually. Ours start at £50 and are produced within 24 hours. See our products.
