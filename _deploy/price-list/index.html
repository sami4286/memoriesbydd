Express Orders

If the Funeral Is Soon

Tell us the date of the service in your first message. We will be honest about whether we can make it, and we will not take an order we cannot deliver in time.

Where the service falls inside our normal turnaround, the order is treated as express and carries a 50% surcharge on the total. That is not a hidden charge — we will tell you before anything is agreed, and confirm the total in writing.

Your proof still comes back in 24–48 hours either way. Express affects printing and delivery, not how quickly you see the design.

Questions

Before You Ask

Is delivery included?

Yes. UK delivery is included in every price on this page. Same-day local delivery is available if you are near us in Stanmore.

What if we need more booklets after the funeral?

We keep your design on file, so a reprint is straightforward. Extra copies are £5.50 each, with a minimum order.

Do you offer trade prices for funeral directors?

Yes — trade pricing, white-label proofs and a dedicated line. See our terms for funeral directors.

How quickly will we see a design?

Your first proof comes back in 24–48 hours from the point we have your photographs and details. See the full timeline.

Do you need paying up front?

Please call us on 0800 023 6263 and we will explain exactly how and when payment works for your order.

Can we pay in instalments?

Talk to us. Funeral costs arrive with no notice, and we would rather have the conversation than have you not ask.

Start with a Conversation

Whichever way is easiest for you. If you would rather speak to someone first, please call — we would much rather you did.
