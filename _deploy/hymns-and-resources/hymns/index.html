Music for the service

Funeral hymns people can sing together.

Familiar music can steady a room. Choose pieces connected to the person, the family’s faith and the atmosphere of the service.

Browse by feeling

Start with what sounds like them.

Check the chosen verses and wording with the officiant or church. Copyrighted lyrics may need permission before they are printed.

A practical number

Two or three hymns is common.

One near the beginning, one after a tribute or sermon, and one near the close often creates a natural rhythm.

Before lyrics go into the booklet

Confirm the exact version, verses and any wording changes with the person leading the service. The same hymn can appear in different editions. We lay out approved lyrics at a comfortable reading size and send every page for proofing before print.
