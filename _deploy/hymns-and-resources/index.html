A simple rule

Choose what sounds like them.

Familiar words often mean more than unusual ones. A hymn the family knows can make the whole room feel involved.

How many hymns and readings are usual?

Most services include two or three hymns: one near the beginning, one after the tribute or sermon, and one near the end. One or two readings are common. There is no fixed rule; the service should reflect the person, the faith tradition and the time available.

We can lay the words out for you

Send the title even if you do not have the words ready. We can help confirm the correct version and arrange verses so guests can follow them comfortably. Nothing is printed until you have approved the complete proof.
