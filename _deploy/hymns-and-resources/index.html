A simple rule

Choose what sounds like them.

Familiar words and music often mean more than unusual ones. The service should feel recognisable to the people in the room.

We can organise incomplete material.

Names, dates, photographs, music and readings can arrive in stages. We bring them into one clear running order, flag what is missing and send every page for approval before printing.
