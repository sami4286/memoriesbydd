For a speaker

Shorter is often kinder.

A reading of one to two minutes can be easier to deliver when emotion is close to the surface.

Layout can make difficult words easier to read

We can use generous line spacing, clear verse breaks and a comfortable type size so the reader does not lose their place. Send the authorised wording exactly as it should appear and the family will approve it in the digital proof.
