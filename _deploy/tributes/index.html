In Loving Memory

Why Memories Exists

A Tribute Worthy of Them

Ashley Whittick lost his mother when he was seven months old. He grew up with her photograph rather than with her, and with the particular kind of absence that never quite becomes normal.

What stayed with him was watching families pull out all the bells and whistles for a wedding — the stationery, the flowers, months of planning — and then send someone they had loved for fifty years off with cheap black and white photocopied paper, folded in half.

It was not about money. Most of the families he met would have paid for something better if anyone had offered it, or shown them what was possible, or simply had the time to sit with them and ask what their mother had actually been like.

So that is what Memories does. Every booklet is designed by hand by someone who has read what you sent and looked at the photographs. There is a person on the end of the phone, from the first call to the last delivery. And nothing is printed until you have seen it and said yes.

“I lost my mother at seven months old. What frustrated me was watching families pull out all the bells and whistles for a wedding, then send their loved ones off with cheap black and white photocopied paper.”

Ashley Whittick, founder

Our Principles

What We Believe

A booklet is kept

Nobody throws away an order of service. It goes in a drawer, a Bible, a box of photographs, and it comes out again at anniversaries. It should be made like something that will be looked at in twenty years, because it will be.

Nobody should feel rushed

Most families come to us with days, not weeks. That is our problem to manage, not yours. You will always know what happens next and when.

Culture is not a detail

A send-off looks different depending on where a family is from. We design for that properly — the flags, the colours, the Nine Night, the tributes from relatives here and at home — rather than treating it as decoration.

The photograph comes first

Everything else on the page is arranged around it. If the only picture you have is creased or faded, send it anyway. We will repair it.
