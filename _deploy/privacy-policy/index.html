Your information

Privacy, explained plainly.

Last updated 29 August 2026. This notice describes the current website and its secure enquiry process, including the service providers used to receive and organise your request.

The short version

We use what you share to answer your questions and prepare your tribute.

We do not sell your information. Ask us at any time what we hold or to delete material that is no longer needed.

Privacy policy

Who is responsible

Memories by DD is responsible for personal information handled through this website and our order process. Contact us at info@memoriesbydd.com, call , or write to Stanmore Place, Stanmore Innovation Centre, Howard Rd, Stanmore HA7 1BT.

What we collect

We may collect your name, phone number, email address, delivery details, service date and venue, information about the deceased, photographs, readings, tributes and other documents you choose to send. We also receive the messages you send by email or secure message.

Why we use it

We use this information to respond to an enquiry, prepare a quote, design and proof a booklet or tribute, arrange printing and delivery, handle changes and provide reprints when requested. Our usual legal basis is taking steps at your request and fulfilling our agreement with you.

Photographs and family material

Please share photographs and documents only where you have authority to do so. They are used to prepare the requested tribute. Tell us if you want the files removed after delivery rather than retained for possible reprints.

Who receives it

Information is shared only where needed to provide the service. Website enquiries are processed by Netlify and organised in Airtable; messages may also pass through our email or secure message providers. Order information is shared with printers and delivery carriers only when required to fulfil your request.

How long we keep it

Enquiry and order records are kept only as long as reasonably needed for the service, accounting and possible reprints. You can ask us to delete design material that is not required for a legal obligation.

Your rights

You may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or a portable copy of your personal information, and object to certain uses. Email us to exercise a right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Cookies and analytics

The current site does not set advertising cookies. If optional analytics are added later, they will not run until any legally required consent has been collected.

Changes

We will update this notice when the order-processing stack changes. The date above shows when it was last revised.
