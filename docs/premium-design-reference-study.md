# Memories by DD — premium website reference study

Research date: 29 August 2026

## The useful lesson

“Million-dollar” does not mean maximum animation. The strongest sites have a clear editorial point of view, exceptional asset treatment, deliberate pacing, simple navigation, and motion that explains hierarchy. The appropriate direction for Memories by DD is **quiet cinema**: emotionally resonant, image-led and tactile, while remaining calm enough for a family arranging a funeral.

## References worth learning from

### Rijksmuseum / Fabrique + Q42

- [European Design Awards case study](https://awards.europeandesign.org/winner/248792)
- [Q42 project case study](https://www.q42.nl/werk/rijks-website)
- [Fabrique’s museum and heritage work](https://www.fabrique.com/our-branches/museums-and-heritage/)

Why it matters: very large imagery, correct responsive cropping, one clear message per image, radically simple navigation, strong typography and deeper story layers. This is the closest strategic reference: memories and cultural artefacts are allowed to carry the experience.

Applied to Memories: full-bleed chapters, fewer competing visuals, transparent product compositions shown at their natural proportions, clear scroll chapters and direct Call / WhatsApp / Start routes.

### Tandjung Sari / Fleava

- [Tandjung Sari story](https://www.tandjungsarihotel.com/en/our-story)
- [Fleava’s awarded profile](https://www.awwwards.com/winner-list/Indonesia)

Why it matters: heritage is presented as lived human history instead of sales copy. Luxury comes from restraint, atmosphere, space and specificity.

Applied to Memories: the founder story is treated as a pivotal editorial chapter, not an “About” card. Copy focuses on what the family feels and needs next.

### Sandooka / Switch & Roy

- [Digital cultural heritage case study](https://www.roy.studio/projects/sandooka)

Why it matters: memories become an explorable, interconnected cultural archive. It demonstrates that emotional value comes from meaningful detail, not generic sentiment.

Applied to Memories: designs are organised by heritage, faith and personality, with distinct imagery and language rather than repeating the same booklet mock-up.

## Motion system for Memories

1. **Cinematic opening:** a short monogram reveal and two-line hero title mask.
2. **Editorial chapter reveals:** display typography cuts into view; body copy arrives softly.
3. **Image curtains:** key photographs reveal with a gold mask and restrained scale change.
4. **Depth, not scroll hijacking:** subtle hero/final-image parallax only on fine-pointer devices.
5. **Process scrollytelling:** the current step gains focus while the progress line and count update.
6. **Tactile product cards:** small elevation, gold edge and pointer light; no fake 3D spin.
7. **Page transitions:** a brief ink-coloured wipe creates continuity between routes.
8. **Reduced-motion version:** all non-essential movement is removed when requested by the operating system.

## Guardrails that keep it premium

- No autoplay audio. A funeral site should never surprise someone with sound.
- No custom scroll hijacking. The visitor stays in control.
- No long loading theatre. Animation must not delay urgent information.
- Animate `transform` and `opacity` where possible; avoid layout-heavy effects. See [web.dev’s animation guidance](https://web.dev/articles/animations-guide).
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, following [W3C guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/animation-from-interactions.html) and [web.dev](https://web.dev/articles/prefers-reduced-motion).
- Treat LCP, INP and CLS as design constraints, not a post-launch cleanup. See [Google’s Core Web Vitals guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/core-web-vitals).

## The emotional conversion logic

The page sequence follows the family’s actual state of mind:

1. Reassurance: “There is a beautiful way to do this.”
2. Meaning: show that this is a keepsake, not disposable stationery.
3. Credibility: explain why the company cares.
4. Self-recognition: let the visitor see a fitting heritage, faith or personality direction.
5. Reduced uncertainty: show four calm steps and a proof-before-print promise.
6. Choice: present packages only after meaning and trust are established.
7. Agency: end with Call, WhatsApp and online start options.

This uses emotional design ethically: it reduces anxiety and decision load rather than manufacturing urgency or exploiting grief.
