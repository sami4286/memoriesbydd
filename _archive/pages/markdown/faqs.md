What does Memories do?
Memories specialise in designing beautiful stylish and classy memorable Funeral Booklets for families all over the world.
Our customers select a product from our ever-growing collection, provide the required content and our expert team produce a Funeral Booklet for their loved one within 24-48 hours.
Customers then get provided a proof which is then put together by our highly skilled finishing department.
What is a ‘Funeral Order Of Service Booklet’, and is it known by any other names?
Funeral Order Of Service Booklets are small booklets/pamphlets that are handed out to guests at a funeral service. They have two main functions; firstly it is a practical way to communicate the order of the service to the guests, and secondly it’s a beautiful keepsake for the guests. Funeral Order Of Service Booklets can also be called; Remembrance Sheets, Funeral Books, Funeral Service Booklets, Service Books, Funeral Bulletin, Funeral Memorial Programs, Funeral Service Programs, Obituary Programs, or Memorial Service Programs.
Who is responsible for creating Funeral Service Booklets?
Typically it is up to the family of the deceased to organise a Funeral Service Booklet, however If you have employed the services of a Funeral Home to help you organise the funeral, the Funeral Director may take responsibility for this.
What are the image requirements?
Each Memories design is unique, and therefore the number and dimensions of images required will differ depending on which style of design you choose. Please refer to the product description of each product to see what is required for each design.
We recommend using 300dpi images, and prefer to receive either jpeg or png images, although we can use other file formats. We understand that sometimes you many not have access to the highest quality images, so we will do our best with what you are able to provide, however all images must be provided in a digital format – printed images must be scanned for uploading.
Please be aware we do accept photos via Whatsapp using the following number 07552916060.
We have hired a Funeral Director, can they create a Funeral Service Booklet on my behalf using Memories?
Yes, Funeral Directors can purchase a service booklet and upload content on behalf of your family, however they are likely to need you to provide them with photos and basic information.
Is there special rates for Funeral Directors?
Yes, we will negotiate alternative pricing for those that use our services regularly, to enquire please email info@memoriesbydd.com
Do I get to see the service booklet before it’s printed?
Yes, absolutely. Once you have provided your project manager with the itinerary of the service, we get straight to work on your Service Booklet design.
On completion of the design and layout, we send you the design by email and Whatsapp for you and the family to review.
What is the revision process?
If after receiving your customised Funeral Service Booklet there are elements that you would like changed, just let us know by email on info@memoriesbydd.com or WhatsApp and we will ensure that the revision is made as quickly as possible.
What happens if I make a mistake in the content I uploaded, or if there is a last minute change to the service booklet?
If after receiving your customised Order Of Service Booklet there are elements that you would like changed, just let us know by email on info@memoriesbydd.com or WhatsApp and we will ensure that the revision is made as quickly as possible.
Can I send a digital copy to people that could not make the service?
We offer extra hard copies which can be mailed direct to those that could not attend, at a unit cost of £5.50 (inc p&p) per booklet.
What if I don't have access to any photos?
We can easily replace photos with patterns, or landscape images as per your preferences.
Can I choose a different design after I’ve purchased one?
If you have not yet supplied us the content for your Funeral Service Booklet, then you may change your mind about the design you wish to use, but must advise us of this when you upload your content. However if you have already supplied us with the content for your Funeral Service Booklet, then we have likely already begun the design process to customise your content using the design you have purchased. To change the design at this point is a considerable amount of rework, therefore you will need to purchase another template, and allow another 24 hours from that point to

<h2>What to write in a funeral programme biography</h2>
<p>Keep it short and heartfelt: name and dates, key life moments, family relationships, passions or achievements, and a closing line that reflects their character.</p>
<h2>What to write in a funeral programme eulogy</h2>
<p>Introduce your relationship, share a few meaningful stories that show their character, express what they meant to others, then close with thanks, hope or faith.</p>
<h2>Short poems and readings for an order of service</h2>
<p>Popular choices include: “Do Not Stand at My Grave and Weep,” “Afterglow,” “When Tomorrow Starts Without Me,” Psalm 23, and “Footprints in the Sand.”</p>
<h2>Best paper sizes and folding options for booklets</h2>
<p>A5 (148 × 210 mm) is most popular. Options: 4-page fold (A4 to A5), 8-page stapled booklets, or 12–16 pages for extra photos; premium 300–350 gsm covers recommended.</p>
<h2>How to design a themed celebration of life programme</h2>
<p>Choose colours and imagery that reflect the person (beach, garden, music). Use clear layouts, consistent fonts, a simple service timeline, photos and a short thank-you note.</p>
<h2>Printable bifold and trifold memorial pamphlet samples</h2>
<p>We offer bifold (A4 folded) and trifold (three-panel) pamphlets with space for a cover photo, order of service and a poem or thank-you message.</p>
<h2>Simple funeral programme layouts for 4-page booklets</h2>
<p>Classic flow: Front cover (photo, name, dates) • Inside left (order of service) • Inside right (hymns/readings/tributes) • Back cover (poem or thank-you).</p>
<h2>Funeral order of service templates with photo collage pages</h2>
<p>Templates include optional collage pages (4–9 images) for family moments and milestones—often as a centre spread or full tribute page.</p>
